Track Your Real Monthly Mobile and Subscription Spending

Small subscriptions add up quickly. A recharge, two OTT apps, a DTH pack and a broadband plan can easily cost thousands of rupees a month. This calculator converts your recharges to a monthly amount and adds all your subscriptions, so you can see your monthly total, yearly total and daily cost.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Enter the price and validity of your mobile recharge (and a second SIM if you have one).
  2. Enter the monthly cost of OTT apps, DTH, broadband and other subscriptions.
  3. Click Calculate to see your monthly, yearly and daily total.
  4. Use the result to decide which subscriptions to cancel or switch.

Formula Used

Monthly Mobile Cost = Price × 30 ÷ Validity Days
Total Monthly = Mobile + OTT + DTH + Broadband + Other
Yearly Total = Monthly Total × 12

Example: A ₹299 plan for 28 days is ₹320 a month. Adding OTT ₹300, broadband ₹500 and DTH ₹250 gives ₹1,370 a month, or ₹16,440 a year.

Money-Saving Tips

  • Cancel OTT apps you have not opened in the last month.
  • Choose recharge plans that bundle OTT apps you already pay for.
  • Share family OTT plans where the terms allow it.
  • Review this total every 3 months.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Why is the recharge converted to 30 days?
Plans have different validity, so converting to a 30-day month lets you add all your costs fairly.
2. Are taxes included?
Only if they are included in the price you enter. Use the final amount you actually pay.
3. Is my data saved?
No. All calculation happens in your browser and nothing is stored.
