Tips for picking the right dragon name

Match the element to the dragon's role in your story. A world-ending threat usually benefits from an Ancient or Storm name - long, weighty, and hard to say quickly. A loyal companion or metallic guardian often reads better with a Celestial or Forest name - softer, easier to shout affectionately across a battlefield. If a generated name is close but not quite right, generate again within the same element; the syllable pool is large enough that a better fit is usually one or two spins away.
