About the Math Solver

Math Solver is a free, browser-based scientific calculator for everyday and exam-level calculations. It runs entirely on your device - nothing you type is sent anywhere, so it works instantly and keeps your calculations private.

What you can calculate

  • Basic arithmetic - addition, subtraction, multiplication, division with correct order of operations
  • Trigonometry - sin, cos and tan, with a Degree/Radian toggle so you can switch modes depending on the problem
  • Logarithms - log (base 10) and ln (natural log)
  • Powers and roots - square (x²), cube (x³), square root (√x), and any custom power using the ^ key
  • Factorials - x! for combinatorics and probability problems
  • Reciprocal - 1/x for quick inverse calculations
  • Constants - π and e available at a tap
  • Memory functions - MC, MR, MS and M+ to store a running value across multiple calculations

How to use it

  1. Tap the number keys to enter your value.
  2. Use +, −, ×, ÷ or ^ for operations, and ( ) to group parts of a longer expression.
  3. For trigonometry, set DEG or RAD first, then tap sin, cos or tan followed by your angle.
  4. Tap = to see the result, or ⌫ to correct a mistake without clearing everything.
  5. Tap C any time to start a fresh calculation.

Frequently asked questions

Is Math Solver free to use?
Yes, it's completely free with no sign-up or download required.

Does it work on mobile?
Yes, the calculator is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets and desktops.

Does it store my calculations?
No. All calculations happen locally in your browser and are not saved or transmitted.

Can I switch between degrees and radians?
Yes, use the DEG/RAD toggle above the display before using sin, cos or tan.
