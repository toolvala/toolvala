How import duty is calculated in India

Duty is charged in layers, each on a different base. IGST is last, and it is charged on the duties too.

What changed with GST 2.0

From 22 September 2025, IGST on imports follows the new GST structure: 5% (essentials), 18% (most goods) and 40% (luxury and sin goods). The old 12% and 28% slabs are gone, so garments, medicines, solar equipment, cars and motorcycles all moved. Always check which slab your HS code now falls in.

Frequently asked questions

How is import duty calculated in India?

Start with the CIF value, add 1% landing charges to get the assessable value, then apply BCD. SWS is 10% of BCD (and AIDC). IGST is applied last on AV + BCD + SWS + any other duties. Landed cost is CIF plus total duty.

What IGST rates apply on imports after GST 2.0?

Since 22 September 2025 the main rates are 5% and 18%, with 40% on luxury and sin goods. A few items stay at 0% or 3% (such as gold). Alcohol for drinking sits outside GST and attracts state levies instead.

What is Social Welfare Surcharge (SWS)?

It is 10% of the Basic Customs Duty, so with 20% BCD it adds 2% of the assessable value, not 10%. Some goods are exempt, which is why you can untick it in the calculator.

Is FOB or CIF used for customs duty?

India assesses duty on CIF. If your supplier quotes FOB, add freight and insurance. When the insurance amount is unknown, 1.125% of FOB is the standard assumption, which this calculator uses if you leave insurance blank.

Can I get a refund of import duty?

GST-registered importers can claim ITC on the IGST paid. Excess payment can be refunded under Section 27 of the Customs Act, and goods re-exported within the permitted period can qualify for drawback under Section 74.

How do FTAs reduce import duty?

Agreements such as India-UAE CEPA and India-ASEAN lower or remove BCD on listed goods when you hold a valid Certificate of Origin. Look up your HS code in the agreement schedule and type that preferential rate into the BCD box. IGST still applies in full.
