1. How to Make a Resume That Gets Read in 2026

In 2026, most companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to sort resumes before a person looks at them. Your resume must be easy for software to read and quick for a recruiter to scan. The Free Resume Maker by Toolvala.in gives you a clean single-column layout with standard headings, so both can understand it.

Recruiters often spend only a few seconds on a first look. A clear structure, short bullet points and measurable results help you pass that first scan. This tool handles spacing, fonts and page breaks, so you can focus on what you wrote. It works for freshers and for experienced professionals.

2. Should You Include a Photo?

It depends on your country and industry:

  • India, Europe, UAE and Asia: A professional photo is common and often expected, especially in customer-facing roles.
  • USA, UK and Canada: Photos are usually left out to avoid bias, except in some creative fields.

This tool makes it your choice. Upload a photo and it appears in the header. Remove it, or never add one, and the header uses the full width for a text-only resume.

3. Passing the ATS Filter

Follow these three rules:

  • Use the job's keywords: If the advert says "Java Programming", write that, not just "Coding".
  • Keep the layout simple: Avoid columns, text boxes and graphics. Our resume is a single column, and the PDF holds real, selectable text for English and other Latin-script content.
  • Use standard headings: "Experience", "Education" and "Skills" are understood by every system. Creative headings can confuse parsers.

Note for Hindi, Marathi and other scripts: if your resume contains non-Latin text, the tool automatically creates the PDF from a picture of the page so every letter displays correctly. That PDF looks perfect, but its text cannot be selected, so use an English version when applying through ATS portals.

4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Is the downloaded PDF ATS-friendly?

Yes, for English and other Latin-script text. The PDF contains real, selectable text in a single-column layout. For Hindi, Marathi and other scripts the tool uses a picture-based PDF, which looks right but is not readable by ATS software.

2. Is my personal data stored?

Nothing is uploaded to our servers. Your text draft is saved only in your own browser so you can continue later. Your photo is not saved. Use "Clear all" to delete the draft at any time.

3. How do I get the best photo quality?

Upload a clear, well-lit headshot. The photo is cropped to a square and resized in your browser, and it is placed in the PDF at good quality.

4. Can I use this for academic CVs?

Yes. You can add as many experience and education blocks as you need, and the PDF continues onto extra pages automatically.

5. Can I edit the PDF after downloading?

PDFs are final documents. Come back to this page, your draft will still be there on the same browser, change what you need and download a new PDF.

6. I am a fresher. What do I put in Experience?

List internships, college projects or freelance work. You can also leave Experience empty and focus on Education, Skills and Certifications. Empty sections are hidden.

7. Should my resume be one page or two?

Freshers and people with under 5 years of experience should aim for one page. Senior professionals with 10+ years can use two pages.

8. Does this tool work on mobile phones?

Yes. Use the Edit and Preview tabs to switch between the form and your resume. You can download the PDF straight from your phone.
