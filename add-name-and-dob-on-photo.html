Add Name and Date on Photo for Exam Forms

For many government exams in India, such as SSC, UPSC, IBPS and RRB, you must upload a passport-size photo with your name and date written on it. The date is usually the DOP (Date of Photo). A photo without these details can get your application rejected.

This tool lets you add name and date on photo online in a few taps, with no Photoshop and no app. It adds a clean strip with your name and DOP or DOB, and keeps the file small enough for the upload limit.

Key Features

  • Name and date on photo: choose DOP, DOB, or date only.
  • Text strip below the photo: your face is never covered. You can also place it over the bottom of the photo if the form asks for that.
  • Reduce KB: set 20, 50, 100 or 200 KB and the tool fits the photo automatically.
  • Custom file name: save the photo as your own name, for example rahul-kumar.jpg.
  • Private: everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Difference Between DOP and DOB

DOP (Date of Photo): the day the photograph was taken. Most exams want a photo that is not older than 3 months.

DOB (Date of Birth): your birth date. Only a few forms ask for it on the photo.

How to Add Name and Date on Photo

  1. Tap Upload Photo and choose your passport-size photo.
  2. Type your full name.
  3. Pick the date (today's date is filled in for you) and choose DOP or DOB.
  4. Set a Max File Size if your form has a KB limit.
  5. Type a file name and tap Download Photo.

Photo Requirements for Major Exams

  • SSC CGL / CHSL: name and date on the photo, size 20KB to 50KB, JPEG format.
  • UPSC: the photo must be recent. Check your notification to see if a name and date are required.
  • Railways (RRB): usually asks for name and date of photo.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add name and date on a photo for SSC?

Upload your photo, type your name, keep the DOP label, set 50 KB (or the limit on your form) and download.

Can I reduce the photo size in KB?

Yes. Enter the maximum KB or tap a quick button. The tool lowers quality and, if needed, size until it fits.

Is my photo safe?

Yes. The photo is processed on your device and never sent to a server.
