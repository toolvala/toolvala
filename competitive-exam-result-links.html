How to Check Your Competitive Exam Result

  1. Open the official website for your specific exam-conducting body from the links above.
  2. Find the notice for your exam and stage (Prelims, Mains, or Final/Interview) - usually listed as a PDF under "Results" or "What's New."
  3. Open the linked PDF - most competitive exam results are published as a roll-number list, not a name-and-marks lookup.
  4. Use your browser's Find function (Ctrl+F or Cmd+F) to search for your roll number within the PDF instead of scrolling through thousands of entries.
  5. Individual marks and cut-offs are often released separately, sometime after the result itself - check the official site's notice board for a follow-up "marks" notification.

Civil Services Exams Are Multi-Stage

UPSC's Civil Services Examination, and most state PSC equivalents, follow a three-stage process: a Preliminary exam (objective, qualifying only), a Mains exam (written, descriptive), and a Personality Test/Interview for those who clear Mains. Each stage has its own separate result, so make sure you're checking the correct stage - clearing Prelims only qualifies you for Mains, it isn't itself a final selection.

Finding Your State's Public Service Commission

Beyond UPSC (the central government's commission) and the three state commissions listed above, every Indian state has its own Public Service Commission (PSC) conducting state-level civil service exams. Most follow a similar official domain pattern - a state PSC's website is almost always a .gov.in or .nic.in domain named after the state (for example, a pattern like "[state]psc.gov.in"). If your state isn't listed on this page, search "[your state] Public Service Commission official website" and look specifically for a government domain rather than a third-party results aggregator.

If the Official Site Won't Load

On result day, official servers receive an extremely high volume of traffic in a short window, so slow loading or timeouts are common. A few things that help:

  • Try again a little later, once the initial traffic spike has passed.
  • Download the result PDF rather than repeatedly refreshing the page.
  • Keep your roll number ready beforehand so you can search the PDF quickly once it loads.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Toolvala.in the official UPSC or state PSC website? ▼
No. Toolvala.in is an independent tool directory. This page only provides direct links to official government websites - your actual result is hosted and issued by UPSC or your state's PSC, not by us.
Why can't I find my name, only a roll number list? ▼
Most competitive exam results are published as a roll-number-only PDF list rather than a name-and-marks lookup. Use your browser's Find function (Ctrl+F) to search for your roll number within the PDF.
What's the difference between Prelims, Mains, and Final results? ▼
Most civil services exams have three stages, each with its own separate result. Clearing Prelims qualifies you for Mains; clearing Mains qualifies you for the Interview/Personality Test, which leads to the Final result.
My state's PSC isn't listed here - where do I check? ▼
Search for your state's Public Service Commission name plus "official website" and look for a .gov.in or .nic.in domain rather than a third-party results aggregator.
Does this page store the details I use to check my result? ▼
No. This page is a static list of official links - Toolvala.in doesn't collect, process, or store any roll number or personal detail you use to check your result on those external sites.
