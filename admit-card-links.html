How to Download Your Admit Card

  1. Open the official website for your specific exam-conducting body from the links above.
  2. Find the "Admit Card" or "Hall Ticket" link, usually on the homepage or under a "Candidate Corner" menu, a few weeks before the exam date.
  3. Log in with your Application/Registration Number and Date of Birth or Password, exactly as used when you applied.
  4. Download the PDF and check every detail carefully before printing - see the checklist below.
  5. Print a clear copy, ideally in color if your admit card includes a photograph, and keep both a physical and digital copy.

Admit Card Checklist - Before You Leave for the Exam

Photo & Signature Check they're clear and match what you uploaded during application.
Exam Centre Address Confirm the exact location and plan your travel time in advance.
Reporting Time Note this is usually earlier than the exam start time - don't confuse the two.
ID Proof Carry the same original photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, etc.) declared in your application.
Personal Details Check your name, date of birth, category, and exam code for any printing errors.
Exam-Specific Rules Some exams specify permitted stationery, dress code, or banned items - read the instructions page on your admit card carefully.

What If There's an Error on Your Admit Card?

If you spot a mistake in your name, photo, category, or other details, most exam bodies provide a short correction window right after admit cards are released - check the official website's notice board for a "Correction" or "Grievance" link specific to your exam. Don't wait until exam day to report an error, since corrections typically can't be made once the exam window has started.

What If Your Admit Card Isn't Available Yet?

Admit cards are typically released a specific number of days before the exam, and the exact date is announced separately from the application deadline. If it isn't showing yet, check the official notice board for the confirmed release date rather than assuming there's a problem with your application - and only worry once that announced date has clearly passed.

If the Official Site Won't Load

Admit card release dates often see a traffic spike similar to result day. A few things that help:

  • Try again a little later, once the initial traffic spike has passed.
  • Keep your application number and password/DOB ready beforehand to avoid repeated failed login attempts.
  • Download and save the PDF immediately once it loads, rather than viewing it repeatedly online.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Toolvala.in the official admit card website? ▼
No. Toolvala.in is an independent tool directory. This page only provides direct links to official exam-body websites - your actual admit card is issued by the relevant board, commission, or university, not by us.
What details do I need to download my admit card? ▼
Typically your Application or Registration Number, along with your Date of Birth or the password you set during application - the exact combination varies slightly by exam body.
Can I enter the exam without a printed admit card? ▼
Almost never - a printed admit card is required at nearly every exam center, along with original photo ID. Always print a copy well before exam day rather than relying on a phone screen.
There's an error on my admit card - what do I do? ▼
Check the official website's notice board for a correction or grievance link specific to your exam, and report it as soon as possible - corrections typically aren't possible once the exam window has begun.
Does this page store my application number or personal details? ▼
No. This page is a static list of official links - Toolvala.in doesn't collect, process, or store any application number or personal detail you use to download your admit card elsewhere.
