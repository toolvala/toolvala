How Much Does Each Member Pay in a Family Plan?

Shared and family plans let several people use one bill. This calculator splits the plan price equally between 2 to 5 members and shows each share per plan cycle, per month and per year. Add the price of a single-line plan to see how much each member saves by sharing.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Enter the total plan price and its validity in days.
  2. Choose the number of members from 2 to 5.
  3. Add a single-line plan price to compare (optional).
  4. Click Calculate to see each member's share and the savings.

Formula Used

Share Per Member = Total Plan Price ÷ Members
Monthly Share = Share × 30 ÷ Validity Days
Yearly Share = Share × 365 ÷ Validity Days

Example: A ₹999 family plan for 28 days split between 4 members is ₹249.75 each per cycle, about ₹267.59 a month. If a single plan costs ₹399, each member saves ₹149.25 per cycle.

Money-Saving Tips

  • Agree who pays and when, so payments are never missed.
  • Check how many extra lines the plan allows and what each line costs.
  • Make sure every member gets the data and calling they need.
  • Compare with separate plans once a year.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Is the split always equal?
This tool splits equally. If members use different amounts, agree on a share and adjust the numbers.
2. How many members can I split between?
From 2 to 5 members.
3. Does it work for any shared bill?
Yes. Any plan with a price and a duration can be split, such as OTT or broadband.
