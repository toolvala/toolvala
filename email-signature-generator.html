How to Add Your Signature

Gmail (desktop)

  1. Tap Copy signature above.
  2. In Gmail, open Settings (gear icon) → See all settings.
  3. Scroll to Signature and choose Create new.
  4. Paste into the box, then press Save changes.

The Gmail mobile app uses the signature you set in Gmail settings on desktop.

Outlook

  1. Tap Copy signature above.
  2. Open Outlook → Settings → Signatures.
  3. Create a new signature and paste.
  4. Set it as the default for new messages and replies.

iPhone (Apple Mail)

  1. Tap Copy signature above on your phone.
  2. Go to Settings → Mail → Signature.
  3. Press and hold in the box, then tap Paste.

If the layout looks wrong, shake your phone to undo the text formatting.

Why Use a Professional Email Signature?

Your email signature appears at the end of every message you send, so it works like a small business card. A clean signature shows your name, role and contact details clearly, helps people trust you, and makes it easy for them to call, email or visit your website.

This tool builds your signature with simple table-based HTML and inline styles. That is the format email apps handle best, so it looks the same in most inboxes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my photo not showing in the email?

Some email apps (like Outlook desktop) block images by default, and some remove images that are embedded in the code. For the most reliable result, host your photo on an https link (for example on your website) and use the Link option. The Initials option also works as a good simple default.

Can I change the size of my photo?

Yes. Use the Photo size slider. The preview updates instantly, and the size is saved in the signature code.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes. It is 100% free, adds no watermark, and your details are never sent to our servers.

How do I crop my photo into a square?

Choose the Upload option and the tool crops your photo to a square automatically. You can also use our Passport Photo Resizer for more control.

Does this work in dark mode?

Email apps handle dark mode in different ways. Dark grey text and a medium theme colour usually stay readable in both light and dark modes. Very light theme colours can be hard to read in dark mode.
