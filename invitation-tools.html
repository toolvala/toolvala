Online invitations for every Indian celebration

Printed cards are costly and slow to deliver, while a link reaches every relative on WhatsApp in seconds. Toolvala invitation tools give you a mobile-first invitation with the details guests actually ask for: date, time, venue, map and a way to reply.

Frequently asked questions

Are the invitation tools free?

Yes. They are free, with no signup and no app to install.

How do I share my invitation?

Fill in your details, tap Copy invitation link and send the link on WhatsApp. Add a guest name to make a personal link for each family.

Where is my data stored?

Not on any server. All details are stored inside the link you copy.

Which languages do the invitations support?

Every invitation has a language switch for English, Marathi, Hindi and Telugu, and you can type names and venues in any of them.

How do guests RSVP?

The RSVP form opens WhatsApp with the reply already written, sent to the number you entered.
