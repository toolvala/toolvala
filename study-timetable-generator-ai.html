How the Study Timetable Generator Works

This tool uses a fixed set of rules, not artificial intelligence. It turns your daily study hours into 25-minute study sessions, shares those sessions between your subjects according to difficulty, and writes out a time-stamped plan for every day until your exam.

  1. Study sessions: your daily hours are cut into 25-minute Pomodoro sessions.
  2. Breaks: a 5-minute break follows each session, and a 15-minute break follows every 4th session. The day never ends on a break.
  3. Time share: sessions are split between weak, medium and strong subjects using the focus mode you pick. If one group is empty, its share is passed to the other groups.
  4. Chapters: inside a group, subjects with more chapters get more sessions.
  5. Rotation: sessions are mixed so the same subject rarely comes twice in a row, and the starting subject changes every day.

With many subjects and few study hours, some subjects may get no session in a day. The tool warns you when this happens. Increase your daily hours or remove a subject to fix it.

Tips for using your timetable

  • Follow the breaks. They are part of the plan.
  • Take a practice test every week and re-rate your weak subjects.
  • Keep time for sleep and meals. Regenerate the plan whenever your exam date or subjects change.
  • Your subject names and settings stay in your browser and are not uploaded.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does the study timetable generator work?

It uses your exam date, daily study hours and subject difficulty to build 25-minute study sessions with breaks, then shares the sessions between subjects by difficulty and rotates the order each day.

Can I give more time to my weak subjects?

Yes. Mark a subject as weak. In Intense focus mode, weak subjects get about 50% of study time, medium 35% and strong 15%.

How do the breaks work?

Each 25-minute session is followed by a 5-minute break. After every 4th session you get a 15-minute break. These are written into the timetable.

Can I download the timetable as a PDF?

Yes. Use Download PDF for the first 30 days. The PDF supports English letters only. If your subject names use another script, such as Marathi or Hindi, the tool opens the Print option instead so you can choose Save as PDF.

What if my exam is very soon?

The plan covers however many days remain. Choose Intense focus so weak subjects get the most sessions.

Is it free, and is my data stored?

It is free with no sign-up. The timetable is built in your browser and your inputs are not uploaded. Like most websites, this page uses Google Analytics and ads, which may set their own cookies.
