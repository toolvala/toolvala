Frequently asked questions

What is the UPI Link Converter?

It turns your UPI ID (for example name@okhdfcbank) and an amount into a clickable payment link or QR text you can share on WhatsApp.

Does the Land Converter support regional units?

Yes. It supports Dismil, Katha, Bigha and Guntha alongside Square Feet and Acres.

Is my JSON code safe?

Yes. The JSON Formatter and SQL converter run in your browser. No code or data is sent to our servers.

How does the Cup to Grams converter work?

Each ingredient has its own density, so 1 cup of flour (about 125 g) and 1 cup of honey (about 340 g) both come out right. You can also pick US, metric, UK or Japanese cup sizes.
