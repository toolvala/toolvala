About the QR Code Scanner

This tool decodes any standard QR code straight in your browser using your device's camera or an image file you already have. No app download, no account, and no data ever leaves your device - the decoding happens locally on your phone or computer.

How to use it

  1. Choose Scan with Camera and tap Start Camera - allow camera access when your browser asks.
  2. Hold the QR code steady inside the green frame. It decodes automatically the moment it's recognized.
  3. Don't have the code in front of you? Switch to Upload Image and pick a photo or screenshot instead.
  4. Once scanned, tap Copy to copy the result, or Open Link if it's a URL.

What it can scan

  • Website URLs and links
  • Wi-Fi login QR codes
  • Plain text and messages
  • Contact card (vCard) QR codes
  • UPI payment QR codes and other text-encoded QR formats

Frequently asked questions

Is this QR scanner free?
Yes, completely free with no limits or sign-up.

Does it work without a camera?
Yes - use the Upload Image tab to scan a QR code from any photo or screenshot.

Is my scan data stored anywhere?
No. Scanning happens entirely in your browser; nothing is sent to Toolvala's servers.

Why isn't my camera starting?
Your browser needs camera permission - check the site permissions in your browser settings and allow camera access, then tap Start Camera again.
