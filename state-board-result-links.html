How to Check Your State Board Result

The exact fields required differ slightly by board, but the general process is the same everywhere:

  1. Open your state's official board link above.
  2. Look for a "Result 2026" or "Class 10 / Class 12 Result" link on the homepage.
  3. Enter your Roll Number (and sometimes date of birth, school code, or application number, depending on the board).
  4. Submit the form to view your result on screen.
  5. Download or screenshot the result for your records - the board's original marksheet is usually issued separately through your school a few days later.

Don't See Your State Listed?

This page covers the boards with the largest number of students, but India has dozens of state and union territory boards. If your state isn't listed above, search "[your state] board official result website" and look specifically for a government (.gov.in, .nic.in) domain or the board's own official site - avoid third-party "result portal" sites that aren't the board itself, since they're not authoritative and sometimes carry outdated or incorrect information.

If the Official Site Won't Load

On result day, official servers receive an extremely high volume of traffic in a short window, so slow loading or timeouts are common. A few things that help:

  • Check if your board also publishes results via DigiLocker, which is usually less congested.
  • Try again a little later in the day, once the initial traffic spike has passed.
  • Avoid refreshing repeatedly, which adds more load - wait a few minutes between attempts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Toolvala.in any state's official board website? ▼
No. Toolvala.in is an independent tool directory. This page only provides direct links to official government and board websites - your actual result is hosted and issued by your respective state board, not by us.
Why do different boards ask for different details? ▼
Each state board runs its own independent result system, so the exact login fields (roll number, date of birth, school code, application number) vary. Your admit card will list exactly what your board requires.
My state isn't listed here - where do I check? ▼
Search for your state board's name plus "official website" and look for a .gov.in or .nic.in domain, or the board's own dedicated site, rather than a third-party results aggregator.
Are these result dates the same every year? ▼
No, each board announces its own result date every year, and they vary by a few weeks from board to board. Always check your specific board's official website or notifications for the confirmed date.
