What is a Gamepad Test?

A Gamepad Test lets you instantly verify that every button, trigger, and joystick on your game controller is responding correctly, using live animated visual feedback powered by your browser's built-in Gamepad API. This free online tool works with Xbox controllers, PlayStation controllers (DualShock/DualSense), and most generic USB or Bluetooth gamepads — simply connect your controller, press a button to activate detection, and start testing every input.

This is especially useful after buying a used controller, troubleshooting stick drift, diagnosing unresponsive buttons, or simply confirming a new controller works correctly before an important gaming session — all without installing any additional software.

Understanding "Stick Drift"

Stick drift is one of the most common and frustrating controller issues, where a joystick registers movement even when you're not touching it. This happens because analog sticks use potentiometers that wear down with use, causing inaccurate readings over time. Our joystick visualization makes stick drift easy to spot — if the dot in the circle isn't perfectly centered when you're not touching the stick, that's a clear sign of drift, and the further off-center it sits at rest, the more pronounced the drift.

Tips for Controller Care and Longevity

  • Avoid excessive force on joysticks: Aggressive, forceful stick movements accelerate wear on the internal potentiometer.
  • Keep the controller clean: Dust and debris around buttons and sticks are common causes of unresponsive or sticky inputs.
  • Update controller firmware: Manufacturers periodically release firmware updates that can improve stick calibration and fix known bugs.
  • Store controllers properly: Avoid extreme heat or leaving controllers in direct sunlight, which can degrade internal components over time.
  • Charge batteries fully and avoid deep discharge cycles: This helps maintain battery health in wireless controllers over the long term.

Frequently Asked Questions

My controller isn't detected - what should I do?

Make sure it's properly connected via USB or paired via Bluetooth, then press any button on the controller, since most browsers require an initial button press to activate gamepad detection.

Does this work with PlayStation and Xbox controllers?

Yes, this test supports most standard controllers including Xbox, PlayStation DualShock/DualSense, and generic USB/Bluetooth gamepads recognized by your browser.

Why does the joystick dot not return exactly to center?

A small offset from center at rest is a sign of stick drift, caused by natural wear in the joystick's internal sensor over time.

Can I test multiple controllers at once?

This test displays the first active controller detected. If you have multiple controllers connected, disconnect the others or press a button on the specific one you want to test.
