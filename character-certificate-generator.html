About the Character Certificate Generator

A character certificate is a short letter that states a person's conduct and character. This free tool by Toolvala.in writes it in the usual Indian format, in English or Marathi, for three common issuers.

  • School or college: for admissions and further education.
  • Employer: for a new job or verification.
  • Gazetted officer or individual: a general good-conduct certificate, often used for police verification, passport or visa.

Where it is asked for

Colleges and universities, employers, police and passport verification, visa applications, and government or competitive exam forms.

Tips

  • Use the issuer's letterhead, a signature and an official seal.
  • Check the person's name and the dates against their records.
  • Some offices ask for their own format. If so, use theirs.
  • Gazetted officer certificates must come from an officer who really knows the person.

FAQ

Is a character certificate the same as a police character certificate?

No. A police clearance (PCC) is issued by the police after verification. This tool prepares a character certificate from a school, employer or individual.

Does it expire?

There is no fixed validity, but many offices want one issued in the last three to six months. Ask for a fresh one if yours is old.
