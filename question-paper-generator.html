Please note: this tool formats the questions you enter. It does not write questions for you, and the sample questions are only examples. Check every question and answer before you use the paper. Your school name, logo and questions stay in your browser and are not uploaded.

How the question paper maker works

  1. School and logo: add your school name and, if you like, your logo. The logo is optional and can be placed on the left or right.
  2. Exam details: choose the exam, subject, class, topic, date and time. The headings can be in English, Hindi or Marathi.
  3. Questions: write one question per line in each section, or load a sample and edit it. Set the marks for each question in a section.
  4. Print: the total marks are counted for you. Tap Print and choose Save as PDF, or print on paper.

How to write each type of question

  • MCQ: Question | option A | option B | option C | option D | B. The last letter is the correct answer and is optional.
  • Fill in the blanks: The sum of angles of a triangle is ____ | 180. The answer after the bar is optional.
  • True or false: The Earth is flat. | F. Use T or F after the bar.
  • Short and long answer: write the question. You can add a model answer after a bar for the answer key.

Tips for a good paper

  • Cover the whole chapter and mix easy, medium and hard questions.
  • Give about one minute for each mark as a rough guide.
  • Read the paper once as a student before you print it.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Does the tool write the questions for me?

No. It formats the questions you type or paste. Three small sample sets are included so you can see how it works. Always check the questions and answers yourself.

Is the logo required?

No. The logo is optional. If you add one, it is shown beside the school name, on the left or the right, and you can remove it at any time.

How are the total marks worked out?

The tool counts the questions in every section and multiplies them by the marks you set for that section, then adds them up. The total always matches the paper.

Can I get the answer key?

Yes. Add the answer after a bar on each line. The key is printed on a separate page, and you can switch it off if you only want the question paper.

Can I save it as a PDF?

Yes. Tap Print and choose "Save as PDF" as the printer in your browser.

Is my data uploaded?

No. Everything is made in your browser. If you tick "Remember", your school name and logo are saved on your device only. Like most websites, this site uses Google Analytics and ads, which may set their own cookies.
