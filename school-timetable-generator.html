About the school timetable template generator

A school timetable shows which subject is taught in each period on every working day. This tool builds a clean landscape A4 timetable for any class, with period timings and breaks worked out automatically from your start time.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make a timetable for a custom class?

Yes. Choose "Custom (type it)" in the class list and type any name, such as 11th Science or B.A. First Year. Nursery to 12th are also in the list.

Can I keep timetables for many classes?

Yes. Save each class on this device, then use Load to open one. Tick "Print all saved classes together" to get one page per class in a single PDF.

How are the period timings set?

Enter the first period start time, the minutes per period, and where the short break and lunch break come. The times for every period are calculated for you.

Can I make it in Marathi?

Yes. Choose Marathi to get day names, class names and labels in Marathi. Subjects are printed exactly as you type them.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is created in your browser. Saved timetables stay in your device storage, and the erase button removes them.
