About the Relieving Letter Generator

A relieving letter is issued by an employer after a resignation is accepted. It confirms the date on which the employee was released from service. New employers usually ask for it during joining and background checks. This free tool by Toolvala.in writes the letter for HR, or writes the request letter an employee sends to HR, in English or Marathi.

Relieving letter or experience letter?

A relieving letter confirms that the company accepted your resignation and released you on a given date. An experience letter states your job title and the period you worked, and many companies add a line on your work. Some companies combine the two. If your new employer wants both, ask for both.

Tips

  • Company letters look best on company letterhead, signed by an authorised person, with a stamp.
  • Employees should return all company assets and complete the exit checklist before asking for the letter.
  • Keep your resignation acceptance email and your last salary slips with the relieving letter.
  • Check the dates against your appointment and resignation letters before you send or sign.

FAQ

Can the company refuse to give a relieving letter?

Companies normally issue it once the notice period and exit formalities are done. If there is a dispute, check your employment contract and speak to HR in writing.

Can I use this letter as an experience letter?

Not always. Many employers want a separate experience letter. Tick "Also request an experience certificate" when you ask HR.
