About these generators

Every tool on this page works the same way. You fill in a short form, the document is written for you in the usual Indian format, and you can download it as a PDF or Word file, print it, or copy the text. Many tools are also available in Marathi. Nothing you type is uploaded, because everything runs in your browser.

These generators prepare drafts for general use. They are not legal advice. Check the rules of your state or office, and use stamp paper, letterhead, signatures or notarisation where required.

FAQ

Are these tools free?

Yes. They are free to use, with no sign-up.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. The letters are built in your browser. Some tools keep a short draft history on your own device only.

Can I use the documents officially?

They are drafts. Most offices accept a properly signed letter, but some need stamp paper, a notary or their own format. Ask the office first.
