About the Appointment Letter Generator

An appointment letter is the formal document that confirms a person's job, pay and terms of employment. This free tool by Toolvala.in helps HR teams and small business owners in India write one in a clear, standard format for a permanent job, a fixed-term contract or an internship. The salary is written in figures and in words, and the letter ends with an acceptance block for the employee.

Offer letter or appointment letter?

An offer letter offers the job with the main terms, and the candidate can accept or decline. An appointment letter is issued when the person joins, and sets out the full terms and conditions. Some companies use one letter for both. This tool works for either.

Tips

  • State the pay clearly and share the salary break-up in a separate sheet.
  • Write the probation and notice terms exactly as your policy says.
  • Give the employee a signed copy and keep the other signed by them.
  • This is a general template, not legal advice. For senior roles or special terms, ask a lawyer or HR consultant to review it.

FAQ

Is an appointment letter mandatory?

It is not a single law for every business, but giving written terms of employment is good practice and protects both sides. Some state shops and establishments rules also expect written appointment terms.

Can I use it for a part-time or contract worker?

Yes. Use "Fixed-term contract" for a contract with an end date, and edit the working hours line for part-time work.
