About the Gap Certificate Generator

Colleges, universities and nursing, polytechnic and professional courses often ask students for a gap certificate when there is a break between their last exam and the new admission. This tool by Toolvala.in writes that statement for you in proper affidavit wording, in English or Marathi, using the dates and reason you enter.

How to use it

  1. Enter your name, age, address and place.
  2. Add your last exam, board and year of passing.
  3. Enter the gap dates and choose the reason.
  4. Check the preview, then download the PDF or Word file.
  5. Print on stamp paper if required, sign it and get it notarised.

Good to know

  • Write the gap honestly. A false affidavit can cancel your admission and has legal consequences.
  • Use your name and board name exactly as on your marksheet.
  • Some colleges give their own gap certificate format. If yours does, use that.
  • Your details stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. This tool is not legal advice.

FAQ

What is a gap certificate?

It is a signed statement that explains a break in your studies, with the dates and the reason. Colleges often ask for it at the time of admission.

Does it need to be notarised?

Many colleges ask for an affidavit on stamp paper sworn before a Notary. Some accept a plain signed declaration. Check with your college.
