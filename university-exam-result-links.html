How to Check Your University Result

The exact process differs by university, but the general steps are similar everywhere:

  1. Open your university's official link above.
  2. Find the "Examination Results" or "Result" section, usually under a "Student Corner," "Examinations," or "News" menu.
  3. Select your specific course and semester (BA, BCom, BSc, BTech, and so on).
  4. Enter your Roll Number, Registration Number, or Hall Ticket Number, along with your date of birth or mother's name where required.
  5. Submit the form to view your provisional marksheet on screen.
  6. Download or screenshot it for your records - remember, the online result is provisional; your university's original marksheet is issued separately.

Provisional vs Original Marksheet

Nearly every Indian university explicitly states that the online result is a provisional document meant for immediate reference only. The original, final marksheet or degree certificate is issued separately by the university's examination department, sometimes weeks or months later, and is the document that actually carries official validity for admissions or employment purposes.

Revaluation and Rechecking

If you're not satisfied with your marks, most universities offer a revaluation or rechecking process, usually open for a short window after the result is declared and requiring a per-subject fee. Check your specific university's examination department page for the exact revaluation deadline and application process, since this varies significantly between universities.

Don't See Your University Listed?

India has hundreds of universities, and this page covers only a handful of the largest ones. If yours isn't listed, search "[your university name] official result website" and look for a domain ending in .ac.in or .edu - these are reserved for genuine academic institutions in India. Avoid third-party "result portal" sites that aren't the university itself, since they can carry outdated or incorrect information, and never enter your details on a site you can't clearly confirm is the university's own.

If the Official Site Won't Load

On result day, official servers receive an extremely high volume of traffic in a short window, so slow loading or timeouts are common. A few things that help:

  • Try again a little later, once the initial traffic spike has passed.
  • Keep your roll number and other required details ready beforehand.
  • Check whether your state's official results portal (like wbresults.nic.in for West Bengal) also mirrors the result, if your university has one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Toolvala.in the official website for these universities? ▼
No. Toolvala.in is an independent tool directory. This page only provides direct links to official university websites - your actual result is hosted and issued by your respective university, not by us.
Is the online result the same as my final marksheet? ▼
No, the online result is provisional and meant for immediate reference. Your university issues the original, final marksheet separately - keep both, but treat the physical original as the authoritative document.
My university isn't listed here - where do I check? ▼
Search for your university's name plus "official result website" and look for a .ac.in or .edu domain rather than a third-party results aggregator.
How do I apply for revaluation if I'm unhappy with my marks? ▼
