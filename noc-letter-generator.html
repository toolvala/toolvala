About the NOC Letter Generator

A No Objection Certificate (NOC) is a short letter in which a person or organisation says it has no objection to something. Offices, embassies, colleges and the RTO often ask for one. This free tool by Toolvala.in writes the letter for you in the usual format, in English or Marathi, for seven common situations.

How to use it

  1. Choose the type of NOC from the list.
  2. Fill in the details that appear for that type.
  3. Add the name and details of the person who will sign it.
  4. Check the preview, then download the PDF or Word file, or copy the text.
  5. Get it signed, and stamped or printed on letterhead if needed.

Good to know

  • An NOC is a request letter. The issuer must agree to it and sign it.
  • Employer and college NOCs look best on letterhead with an official seal.
  • Property NOCs give up a right, so read every line before signing. A lawyer can check it.
  • For a vehicle transfer, the RTO may also ask for its own forms along with this letter.

FAQ

Who writes the NOC?

The person or organisation whose permission is needed. For example, an employee asks the employer, and the employer signs it.

Does an NOC expire?

It has no fixed validity, but the office that asked for it may want a recent date. Ask for a fresh one if it is old.
