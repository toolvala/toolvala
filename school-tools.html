How these tools work

  1. Open the tool and enter your UDISE number and school name. Saved details fill in next time on the same device.
  2. Fill in the student, class or exam details and choose English or Marathi.
  3. Check the preview, then download the PDF or print it. Add the signature and seal of the authorised person.

What they have in common

  • Free, with no sign-up
  • English and Marathi (मराठी)
  • A4 layout that fits on one page, with a few design styles on some tools
  • Everything is created in your browser, and saved records stay on your own device
Please note: these tools help authorised school and college staff prepare documents from information they already hold. They are not official document services and are not linked to any government body, board or university. You are responsible for the accuracy of what you enter, and a document is valid only with the signature and seal of the authorised signatory.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. All five tools are free and work without signing in.

Are my school and student details uploaded?

No. The documents are created in your browser, and saved records stay in your device storage. Like most websites, this site uses Google Analytics and ads, which may set their own cookies.

Can I use them in Marathi?

Yes. Each tool lets you choose Marathi for labels and headings. Names and other details are printed exactly as you type them.

Can I use them on a phone?

Yes. The forms are built for mobile screens, and the preview scales to fit.
