Online Biodata Maker: Create a Marriage or Job Biodata for Free

A biodata (biographical data) is often your first impression, whether you are applying for a job or sharing a proposal for marriage. Unlike a CV, a biodata puts personal details, family background and basic qualifications in one clear page. This free biodata maker gives you a neat format in minutes, with no Word or Photoshop needed.

What to Include in a Marriage Biodata

A good marriage biodata has your name and photo, date, time and place of birth, height, education, occupation, family details (parents, siblings, native place), religion and community, contact details and what you expect from a partner. Use "Extra Details" for items such as Gotra, Rashi or Nakshatra.

Difference Between Resume, CV and Biodata

  1. Resume: a short summary of work experience and skills.
  2. CV (Curriculum Vitae): a longer academic and professional history.
  3. Biodata: focuses on personal details such as date of birth, marital status and family background. It is common in South Asia for marriage proposals and some job and government forms.
