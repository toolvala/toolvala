About the Rent Agreement Generator

This free tool by Toolvala.in prepares a residential rent agreement or leave and licence agreement in clear legal wording. It covers the parties, property, term, rent in figures and words, deposit, maintenance, lock-in, notice, renewal increase, vacating, police verification and jurisdiction.

The 11 month standard

Under the Registration Act, a lease of more than 12 months must be registered, so many landlords use 11 months. State rules differ. In Maharashtra, for example, leave and licence agreements are normally registered and stamped even when they are under 12 months. Check your state's rules before you sign.

To make it enforceable

  • Print it on stamp paper of the correct value for your state.
  • Both parties sign every page, and two witnesses sign at the end.
  • Register it if your state requires it. Many states now offer online registration.
  • Keep copies of both parties' ID proof and the rent receipts.

This tool prepares a draft only and is not legal advice. For high-value or unusual arrangements, ask a lawyer to review it.

FAQ

Rent agreement or leave and licence?

A lease gives the tenant an interest in the property. A leave and licence only gives permission to occupy it for a fee. Both are used for residential rentals, and the one commonly used depends on your state and city.

Can the rent be increased?

Only as the agreement says. This tool lets you add a fixed percentage increase on renewal.
