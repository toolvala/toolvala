How to Check Your RRB Result

  1. Open the official RRB portal (rrb.indianrailways.gov.in) or the application portal (rrbapply.gov.in) from the links above.
  2. Find the notice for your specific exam using its CEN number (Centralized Employment Notice number, printed on your admit card and application).
  3. Open the linked result PDF or scorecard section for your exam's current stage (CBT-1, CBT-2, Document Verification, or Medical Examination).
  4. Use your browser's Find function (Ctrl+F or Cmd+F) to search for your roll number within the PDF list instead of scrolling through thousands of entries.
  5. For scorecards specifically, log in to your candidate account using your registration details from the application.

Why the Website Recently Changed

Until recently, each of India's 21 Railway Recruitment Boards (Mumbai, Chennai, Chandigarh, Bhubaneswar, and others) ran its own separate regional website, which made it confusing to know exactly where to check a result depending on which zone you applied through. RRB has now merged all of these into the single unified portal at rrb.indianrailways.gov.in, with the older regional sites being phased out. If you bookmarked an old regional RRB site, switch to the new unified portal or the application portal above.

Understanding CEN Numbers

Every RRB recruitment notification is issued under a specific CEN (Centralized Employment Notice) number, such as "CEN 01/2025" or "CEN 05/2025" - different CENs cover different exam categories (NTPC, Group D, ALP, and so on). When searching for your result, match the CEN number on your admit card to the correct notice on the official portal, since multiple CENs are often active and updating at the same time.

Multi-Stage Exam Process

Most RRB exams are conducted in stages - typically a first computer-based test (CBT-1), followed by a second computer-based test (CBT-2) for shortlisted candidates, and then Document Verification and a Medical Examination for final selection. Each stage has its own separate result, so make sure you're checking the correct stage for your specific exam and CEN.

If the Official Site Won't Load

On result day, official servers receive an extremely high volume of traffic in a short window, so slow loading or timeouts are common. A few things that help:

  • Try again a little later, once the initial traffic spike has passed.
  • Download the result PDF rather than repeatedly refreshing the page.
  • Keep your CEN number and roll number ready beforehand so you're not searching for them while the site is under heavy load.

What is a CEN number? ▼
