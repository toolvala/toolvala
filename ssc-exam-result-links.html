How to Check Your SSC Result

Unlike school board results, most SSC results are published as a PDF list of roll numbers (not an individual name-and-marks lookup), so the process is a little different:

  1. Open the official SSC portal and go to the "Results" section.
  2. Find the notice for your specific exam and tier (for example, "CGL 2026 Tier-1 Result").
  3. Open the linked PDF - it lists the roll numbers of candidates who qualified.
  4. Use your browser or PDF reader's Find/Search (Ctrl+F or Cmd+F) function and type your roll number to locate it quickly instead of scrolling through thousands of entries.
  5. Some exams also publish an individual scorecard separately on the candidate login/portal - check both the PDF list and your candidate login.

SSC Exams Are Multi-Tier

Most SSC exams (like CGL and CHSL) are conducted in multiple tiers or stages - for example, a Tier-1 computer-based test followed by a Tier-2 test, and sometimes a skill or typing test after that. Each tier has its own separate result, released on its own date, so make sure you're checking the result for the correct tier of your exam.

Answer Key and Objection Window

Before the final result, SSC typically publishes a provisional answer key and opens a short window (often a few days) during which candidates can raise objections to specific questions, usually for a small fee per objection. The final result reflects any changes made after this review. Check the official portal's notice board for the exact objection window dates for your exam.

Regional SSC Offices

SSC conducts exams through several regional offices across India (Northern, Southern, Western, Central, Eastern, North Eastern, and others), and some region-specific notices or results may be published on the relevant regional office's website in addition to the main national portal. If you can't find your result on the main site, check whether your exam center falls under a specific regional office and look for that office's own website.

If the Official Site Won't Load

On result day, official servers receive an extremely high volume of traffic in a short window, so slow loading or timeouts are common. A few things that help:

  • Try again a little later, once the initial traffic spike has passed.
  • Download the PDF result list rather than repeatedly refreshing the page.
  • Check your candidate login/dashboard separately, since it sometimes loads faster than the public results page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Toolvala.in the official SSC website? ▼
No. Toolvala.in is an independent tool directory. This page only provides direct links to the official SSC and government websites - your actual result is hosted and issued by SSC, not by us.
Why can't I find my name in the result, only roll numbers? ▼
Most SSC results are published as a roll-number-only PDF list rather than a name-and-marks lookup. Use your browser's Find function (Ctrl+F) to search for your roll number within the PDF.
What's the difference between Tier-1 and Tier-2 results? ▼
Most SSC exams have multiple stages (tiers), each with its own separate result. Qualifying Tier-1 lets you appear for Tier-2, and so on - make sure you're checking the result for the correct tier of your specific exam.
Where can I raise an objection to the answer key? ▼
SSC typically opens a short objection window after publishing the provisional answer key, accessible through the official candidate portal. Check the notice board on ssc.gov.in for the exact dates for your exam.
Does it store the details I search for? ▼
No. This page is a static list of official links - Toolvala.in doesn't collect, process, or store any roll number or personal detail you use to check your result elsewhere.
