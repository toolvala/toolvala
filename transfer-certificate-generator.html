About this Transfer Certificate maker

A Transfer Certificate (TC), also called a School Leaving Certificate, records a student's details and school history when they leave a school. The next school asks for it at admission. This tool builds it from your school's own records. The table format identifies the school by its UDISE number and ties the certificate to the student's register entry. The school-register format follows the common school-office layout, with the Admission number, Admission date and Discharge number at the top, and optional Student ID, Aadhaar and PEN lines.

Frequently asked questions

Which format should I use?

Use the table format for a numbered, table-style certificate. Use the school-register format if your office uses the label-and-value layout with Admission and Discharge numbers and Class Teacher, Writer and Headmaster signatures. If you are not sure, ask the school that will receive the certificate.

Can I add the PEN number to the Transfer Certificate?

Yes. In the table format, fill in the PEN box. In the school-register format, tick Add PEN number and type it. If you tick it but leave the box empty, a blank line is printed for you to fill by hand.

Does it fetch school details from the UDISE number?

There is no public UDISE lookup that a website can call, so the school name and address are typed once. They are then remembered on your device against that UDISE number and filled in automatically next time.

Is student data uploaded anywhere?

No. The certificate is built entirely in your browser. Saved details stay in your device's storage, and the erase button removes them.

Can I get the certificate in Marathi?

Yes. Pick Marathi under certificate language and the whole certificate, including the date of birth in words, switches to Marathi.

Is the Aadhaar number checked?

The tool checks that it has 12 digits and shows a warning if it fails the standard Aadhaar checksum, which catches most typing mistakes. It cannot confirm that the number belongs to the student.

What is the Student ID or Saral ID field?

It is an optional field for the ID your school or state portal gave the student, such as the Saral ID. If you fill it in, it is printed on the certificate. Leave it empty and it is left out.

Is the certificate valid without a signature?

No. Print it, then have it signed by the Headmaster or Principal and stamped with the school seal.
