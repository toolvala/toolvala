About this bonafide certificate maker

A bonafide certificate confirms that a student is genuinely enrolled in a school or college. It is commonly needed for scholarships, bank accounts, passports, bus passes and competitive exam forms. The Detailed format builds the certificate from your school's own records: the UDISE number identifies the school, and the Admission/Withdrawal (General) Register number ties the certificate to the student's entry in the school register. The Simple format is a short one-page landscape certificate, like the plain printed formats many offices accept, with blank lines that you can fill by hand.

Frequently asked questions

Which format should I use, Detailed or Simple?

Use Detailed when the office asks for UDISE, register number, PEN or Aadhaar details. Use Simple for a short standard bonafide letter. If you are not sure, ask the office that needs the certificate which details it requires.

Does it fetch school details from the UDISE number?

There is no public UDISE lookup that a website can call, so the school name and address are typed once. They are then remembered on your device against that UDISE number and filled in automatically next time.

Is student data uploaded anywhere?

No. The certificate is built entirely in your browser. Saved details stay in your device's storage, and the erase button removes them.

Can I get the certificate in Marathi?

Yes. Pick Marathi under certificate language and the whole certificate switches to Marathi, in both the Detailed and Simple formats.

Can I print the Simple certificate with blank lines?

Yes. Leave any box empty and a blank line is printed in its place, so you can write the details by hand.

Can I add Aadhaar and PEN to the Simple certificate?

Yes. Tick Add PEN or Add Aadhaar number in the Simple tab. The number is printed in a line under the main statement, and you can hide the first 8 digits of the Aadhaar number. If you tick the option but leave the box empty, a blank line is printed for you to fill by hand.

Is the Aadhaar number checked?

In the Detailed format, the tool checks that it has 12 digits and passes the standard Aadhaar checksum, which catches most typing mistakes. It cannot confirm that the number belongs to the student.

Is the certificate valid without a signature?

No. Print it, then have it signed by the Headmaster or Principal and stamped with the school seal.
