About this bonafide certificate maker

A bonafide certificate confirms that a student is genuinely enrolled in a school. It is commonly needed for scholarships, bank accounts, passports, bus passes and competitive exam forms. This tool builds the certificate from your school's own records: the UDISE number identifies the school, and the Admission/Withdrawal (General) Register number ties the certificate to the student's entry in the school register.

Frequently asked questions

Does it fetch school details from the UDISE number?

There is no public UDISE lookup that a website can call, so the school name and address are typed once. They are then remembered on your device against that UDISE number and filled in automatically next time.

Is student data uploaded anywhere?

No. The certificate is built entirely in your browser. Saved details stay in your device's storage, and the erase button removes them.

Can I get the certificate in Marathi?

Yes. Pick Marathi under certificate language and the whole certificate switches to Marathi.

Is the Aadhaar number checked?

The tool checks that it has 12 digits and passes the standard Aadhaar checksum, which catches most typing mistakes. It cannot confirm that the number belongs to the student.

Is the certificate valid without a signature?

No. Print it, then have it signed by the Headmaster or Principal and stamped with the school seal.
