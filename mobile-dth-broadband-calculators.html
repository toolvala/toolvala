Know What Your Mobile, DTH and Broadband Really Cost

Recharge packs, DTH balances and broadband plans are sold with different prices, validity periods and data limits, so they are hard to compare. These calculators turn each plan into a simple cost per day, per month or per year, and show the exact day it expires.

Which Calculator Do You Need?

  • Plan expiry date: use the Mobile Recharge Validity Calculator or the DTH Recharge Expiry Calculator.
  • Comparing plans: use Cost Per Day, the 28 Day vs 30 Day Yearly Cost, or Prepaid vs Postpaid.
  • Data and internet: use Data Per Day or the Broadband Cost Per GB Calculator.
  • Monthly spending: use the Monthly Mobile, OTT & Internet Expense Calculator or the Family Plan Split Calculator.

More date tools are in Expiry Date Tools, and money tools are in Finance Calculators. You can also browse All Tools.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Which calculator should I start with?
Start with the Mobile Recharge Validity Calculator to find your plan expiry date and cost per day. Then use the Cost Per Day or Yearly Cost calculators to compare plans.
2. Can I compare prepaid and postpaid plans?
Yes. The Prepaid vs Postpaid Calculator turns both into a yearly cost so you can see which is cheaper.
3. Do these tools work for DTH and broadband too?
Yes. The DTH Recharge Expiry Calculator and the Broadband Plan Cost Calculator are made for those services.
4. Are the results exact?
They are estimates based on the numbers you enter. Always check the final price and validity in your operator app.
5. Is anything I enter saved?
No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is stored.
