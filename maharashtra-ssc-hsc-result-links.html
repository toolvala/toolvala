How to Check Your Maharashtra Board Result

  1. Open one of the official links above (try an alternate if the first one is slow to load).
  2. Click the "SSC Result" or "HSC Result" link for the current year shown on the homepage.
  3. Enter your Seat Number (roll number, printed on your hall ticket/admit card).
  4. Enter your Mother's First Name exactly as required on the form.
  5. Submit the form to view your result on screen.
  6. Save a screenshot or download the PDF marksheet for your records.

Other Ways to Get Your Result

  • DigiLocker: Your digitally signed marksheet is typically made available on DigiLocker after declaration, and is accepted as a legally valid document.
  • SMS: The board has enabled SMS-based result checking in past years for select exams - check the official website's notice board close to result day to see if it's active for the current year.
  • Your school: Schools usually receive the original physical marksheet a few days after the online result is declared.

If the Official Site Won't Load

On result day, official servers receive an extremely high volume of traffic in a short window, so slow loading or timeouts are common and expected - it's rarely a problem with your own device or connection. A few things that help:

  • Try an alternate link from the list above instead of refreshing the same one repeatedly.
  • Try again a little later in the day, once the initial traffic spike has passed.
  • Check your result via DigiLocker if it has been published there.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Toolvala.in the official Maharashtra board website? ▼
No. Toolvala.in is an independent tool directory. This page only provides direct links to the official government and board websites - your actual result is hosted and issued by MSBSHSE, not by us.
What do I need to check my result? ▼
Your Seat Number (roll number) from your hall ticket, and your Mother's First Name, exactly as required by the official result form.
Why is the official site slow or not loading on result day? ▼
Millions of students try to check their result in the same short window, which puts heavy load on the official servers. Try an alternate link, or check again a little later.
Is the DigiLocker marksheet valid? ▼
Yes, digitally signed marksheets issued via DigiLocker are treated as legally valid documents, though you should still confirm with your school if you need the original physical copy.
Where can I download my admit card / hall ticket? ▼
Admit cards are issued through your school via the official MSBSHSE website, mahahsscboard.in. Contact your school if you haven't received yours.
