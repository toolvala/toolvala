What is a Microphone Test?

A Microphone Test lets you instantly verify that your mic is detected and picking up sound, using a real-time animated volume meter and waveform visualization powered by your browser's Web Audio API. This free online tool requires no downloads or installations — simply grant microphone access and start speaking to see your voice level respond live on screen, confirming your microphone is working correctly before an important video call, podcast recording, or livestream.

Just like our webcam test, your audio is processed entirely within your browser and is never uploaded, recorded, or transmitted anywhere. The moment you stop the test or leave this page, microphone access ends completely.

Tips for Better Microphone Audio Quality

  • Position the mic correctly: Most mics perform best 6-12 inches from your mouth, slightly off-axis to reduce plosive sounds ("p" and "b" pops).
  • Reduce background noise: Close windows, turn off fans, and choose a quiet room when possible.
  • Use a pop filter or foam windscreen: These reduce harsh breathing and plosive sounds for cleaner audio.
  • Check input levels before recording: Aim for a level that peaks without clipping (distorting) during your loudest moments.
  • Avoid touching the mic or cable: Physical vibration and handling noise are common sources of unwanted audio artifacts.

Built-in vs External Microphones

Laptop and webcam built-in microphones are convenient but generally pick up more ambient room noise and offer less clarity than a dedicated USB or XLR microphone. If this test shows your built-in mic working but audio quality still sounds thin or noisy during calls, consider testing with an external microphone instead — the difference in clarity is often substantial for video calls, podcasting, or content creation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my voice recorded during this test?

No. Audio is analyzed in real time directly in your browser for the visual meter only — nothing is recorded, saved, or transmitted anywhere.

Why isn't the meter responding to my voice?

Check that you've granted microphone permission to this site, and confirm the correct microphone is selected as your system's default input device.

My mic works here but not in my video call app - why?

This usually means the specific app doesn't have microphone permission enabled, or a different input device is selected within that app's own audio settings.

Can I test a Bluetooth microphone/headset?

Yes, as long as your Bluetooth device is properly paired and selected as the active input device on your computer or phone.
