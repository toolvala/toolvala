How to Take a Perfect Photo at Home

  1. Lighting: Face a window for soft, even light. Avoid shadows on your face or the wall.
  2. Background: Stand in front of a plain white wall or sheet.
  3. Position: Stand about 1 metre from the background with the camera at eye level.
  4. Expression: Neutral face, mouth closed, eyes open and looking straight ahead.
  5. Attire: Wear dark clothes that contrast with the white background. Avoid glare on glasses.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How do I resize a passport size photo to 3.5 x 4.5 cm?

Upload your photo, keep the India Passport preset (photo width 3.5 and height 4.5 cm) and click Resize & Download.

2. How can I reduce passport photo size in KB?

Enter the maximum KB in the Max File Size box. The tool lowers the JPG quality step by step until the photo fits under your limit.

3. Can I change the downloaded file name?

Yes. Type any name in the File Name box and your photo is saved as name.jpg.

4. Why was my photo rejected?

Common reasons are shadows on the face, glare on glasses, a background that is not plain white, or incorrect dimensions. Make sure your source photo is clear.

5. Does this tool change the background?

No. It resizes, crops and compresses. Transparent areas are filled with white, but the background of your photo should already be correct.

6. What is the DPI for passport photos?

Most online applications need 300 DPI. This tool renders your chosen size at 300 DPI.
