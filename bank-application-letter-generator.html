About the Bank Application Letter Generator

Banks in India accept a simple signed letter for many everyday requests. This free tool by Toolvala.in writes that letter for you, addressed to the Branch Manager, with your account details in the usual format. It covers account closing, debit card requests, cheque books, KYC updates, address changes and account statements, in English or Marathi.

Tips for bank letters

  • Sign the way you signed when you opened the account. Banks match it with their records.
  • Write the full account number, and carry your passbook and an original photo ID to the branch.
  • Many banks have their own printed form for closing an account, KYC or a new card. If the branch gives you one, use that. This letter can go with it.
  • If your card is lost or stolen, block it at once through the bank's app, SMS or helpline. Do not wait to hand in a letter.
  • Ask for an acknowledgement copy of your letter.

FAQ

Can I send the letter by email?

Some banks accept requests from your registered email, but many need a signed letter at the branch. Check with your bank.

Is a letter enough to close my account?

Usually the branch also asks for a form, your passbook, unused cheque leaves, your debit card and ID proof. Carry them all.
