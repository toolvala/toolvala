How to Check Your CBSE Result

  1. Open one of the official links above (try an alternate if the first one is slow to load).
  2. Click the "CBSE Result 2026" link for Class 10 or Class 12 on the homepage.
  3. Enter your Roll Number, School Number, Centre Number, and Admit Card ID exactly as printed on your admit card.
  4. Submit the form to view your result on screen.
  5. Download and print the result for your records - remember, the board's original marksheet is issued separately through your school.

Check Your Result via SMS

CBSE has offered SMS-based result checking in past years. The typical format (confirm on the official site that it's active for the current year before sending):

  • Class 10: Type CBSE10 (roll number) (date of birth) (school number) (centre number) and send to 7738299899
  • Class 12: Type CBSE12 (roll number) (date of birth) (school number) (centre number) and send to 7738299899

Check Your Result via IVRS (Phone Call)

CBSE has also offered a phone-based Interactive Voice Response System (IVRS) in past years, typically reached by dialing your area's STD code followed by 24300699, charged at a small per-minute rate. Confirm this is active for the current year on the official CBSE website before calling.

Other Ways to Get Your Result

  • DigiLocker: Your digitally signed marksheet is typically made available on DigiLocker after declaration and is accepted as a legally valid document.
  • Umang App: The government's Umang app has also offered a direct link to check CBSE results in past years.
  • Your school: Schools usually receive the original physical marksheet a few days after the online result is declared.

Two-Phase Class 10 Exams (Recent NEP Change)

Following the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 reforms, CBSE has introduced two board exams per year for Class 10 in recent cycles - a Phase 1 and an optional Phase 2 for students who want to reappear or improve their score. If you appeared in both phases, check the official website carefully for which result window applies to you, since Phase 1 and Phase 2 results are typically declared on different dates.

If the Official Site Won't Load

On result day, official servers receive an extremely high volume of traffic in a short window, so slow loading or timeouts are common and expected. A few things that help:

  • Try an alternate link from the list above instead of refreshing the same one repeatedly.
  • Try the SMS or IVRS method instead of the website.
  • Check DigiLocker or the Umang app if your result has been published there.
  • Try again a little later in the day, once the initial traffic spike has passed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Toolvala.in the official CBSE website? ▼
No. Toolvala.in is an independent tool directory. This page only provides direct links to the official CBSE and government websites - your actual result is hosted and issued by CBSE, not by us.
What details do I need to check my CBSE result? ▼
Your Roll Number, School Number, Centre Number, and Admit Card ID, all printed on your admit card.
Can I check my result without internet access? ▼
Yes - CBSE has offered both SMS and IVRS (phone call) methods in past years as alternatives to the website. Confirm these are active for the current year on the official site before using them.
Is the DigiLocker marksheet valid? ▼
Yes, digitally signed marksheets issued via DigiLocker are treated as legally valid documents, though you should still confirm with your school if you need the original physical copy.
What if I appeared for both Phase 1 and Phase 2 of Class 10? ▼
Phase 1 and Phase 2 results are typically declared separately, on different dates. Check the official CBSE website for which result window and link applies to your specific phase.
