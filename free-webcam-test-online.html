What is a Webcam Test?

A Webcam Test lets you instantly check whether your camera is working correctly directly in your browser, without downloading any software or creating an account. This free online webcam test uses your browser's built-in camera access permission (the same technology used by video call apps) to show a live preview, so you can confirm your webcam is detected, positioned correctly, and producing a clear picture before an important video call, livestream, or recording session.

This tool is completely private — your video feed is processed entirely within your own browser and is never uploaded, recorded, or sent to any server. You're always in control, and you can stop the camera or revoke permission at any time through your browser settings.

Tips for Better Webcam Video Quality

  • Light your face, not your background: Position a light source in front of you rather than behind you to avoid silhouette effects.
  • Position the camera at eye level: Avoid extreme upward or downward angles for a more natural, professional look.
  • Close bandwidth-heavy apps: Other applications competing for camera or CPU resources can reduce frame rate during calls.
  • Clean the lens regularly: A quick wipe with a microfiber cloth can noticeably improve image clarity.
  • Check your resolution settings: Some video call apps default to lower resolution — verify your camera's maximum resolution using this test.

Why Test Your Webcam Before a Call

Testing your webcam in advance — before a job interview, client meeting, or livestream — helps you catch issues like poor lighting, an obstructed lens, or driver problems while you still have time to fix them. It only takes a few seconds and can save you from an awkward "can you see me?" moment during an important call.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my video recorded or uploaded during this test?

No. Your webcam feed is processed entirely in your browser and is never transmitted, recorded, or stored anywhere — closing or leaving this page instantly ends the camera access.

Why does my browser ask for camera permission?

Browsers require explicit permission before any website can access your camera, as a privacy and security safeguard. You can revoke this permission anytime in your browser settings.

My webcam works in this test but not in my video call app - why?

This usually means the video call app doesn't have camera permission enabled separately, or another application is already using the camera exclusively. Check the app's own camera settings.

Can I test an external USB webcam?

Yes, if your external webcam is properly connected and has working drivers, it will appear as a selectable camera option when you start the test.
